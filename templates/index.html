About Zextrex Technology

An AI company built by two engineers who kept meeting the same problem: teams surrounded by data and still doing the thinking by hand.

Zextrex Technology was founded by Muhammad Moiz and Muhammad Qasim to close that gap. We design and deploy applied artificial intelligence assistants that hold a real conversation, models that see what is coming, and automation that takes the repetitive middle out of a workflow. Everything we ship is built on the client's own data and measured against the client's own numbers.

We work in small, senior teams. A founder stays on every project from the first conversation to the final deployment, which is why our scope documents are short and our timelines hold. We would rather hand over a narrow system that works on Monday than a wide one that demos well and stalls in production.

Our work reaches customer support, education, commerce, logistics and healthcare operations anywhere a decision repeats often enough that a machine can learn its shape. The assistant on this page, Zextrex, is the same technology we build for clients, pointed at our own company.

Services

What we can build for you

Six services, each sold as a working system rather than a retainer. Every engagement starts with a two-week discovery and ends with something in production.

Conversational AI assistants

Chat, voice and in-app assistants that hold a thread

We build assistants that know your product, your policies and your tone the way Zextrex knows this company. They answer customers, guide staff through procedures, and hand over to a human the moment a conversation needs one.

  • Website, WhatsApp, mobile app and voice channels
  • Grounded on your documents, so answers cite your own material
  • Escalation rules, transcripts and quality review built in
  • Multilingual, including English and Urdu
Typical delivery: 3–6 weeks · Ongoing tuning available

Predictive systems

Forecasting, scoring and anomaly detection

Your history already contains the pattern. We turn it into a model that estimates demand, flags risk, ranks leads or catches the transaction that does not belong then wrap it in an API your existing software can call.

  • Demand and inventory forecasting
  • Churn, credit and lead scoring
  • Fraud and anomaly detection
  • Accuracy reported against a baseline you agree to up front
Typical delivery: 4–8 weeks · Requires historical data

Workflow automation

The repetitive middle, handled end to end

Invoices read, tickets routed, applications screened, reports written. We map the process first, automate only the parts that are safe to automate, and leave a clear audit trail for the rest.

  • Document understanding for invoices, forms and contracts
  • Email and ticket triage with automatic routing
  • Scheduled reporting and summarisation
  • Human approval steps wherever a decision carries weight
Typical delivery: 3–5 weeks

Custom AI product development

From idea to a shipped application

When the AI is the product, not a feature. We take a concept through prototype, user testing and launch interface, backend, model layer and deployment with a founder on the build throughout.

  • Web and mobile applications with AI at the core
  • Model selection, prompt and retrieval architecture
  • Usage analytics, cost control and scaling plan
Typical delivery: 8–16 weeks

Data engineering and integration

The groundwork intelligence stands on

Models fail on messy inputs long before they fail on mathematics. We clean, connect and structure your data, then keep it flowing so the system you paid for is still accurate a year later.

  • Pipelines from your CRM, ERP, database or spreadsheets
  • Cleaning, labelling and dataset preparation
  • Monitoring, retraining schedules and drift alerts
Typical delivery: 2–6 weeks

AI consulting and applied research

Decide what to build before building it

A short, honest engagement for teams weighing options. We audit where AI would actually pay back, benchmark the approaches, and write the plan including the cases where our recommendation is to do nothing yet.

  • Opportunity audit and prioritised roadmap
  • Model benchmarking and cost comparison
  • Safety, privacy and evaluation review
  • Team training and handover documentation
Typical delivery: 2–3 weeks
